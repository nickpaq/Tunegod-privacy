1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the TuneGod mobile application for iPhone (the “App”) handles information. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to Nicholas Kenneth Paquette, the developer of TuneGod, and “you” refers to the person using the App.

By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not ask you to create an account, and it does not send any information about you or your device to us or to anyone else. In particular, we do not collect:

  • your name, email address, phone number or other contact details;
  • your location;
  • device identifiers, advertising identifiers or IP addresses;
  • usage data, analytics or crash data (except as described under Apple below, if you choose to share it);
  • your audio, recordings, projects or files.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

To work, the App saves the following on your device only. This information never leaves your device unless you choose to share it.

  • Projects and recordings. Your sounds, patterns, recordings and projects (including .tg project files) are saved in the App's private storage on your iPhone.
  • Favourites. Sounds you mark as favourites are copied into the App's storage so they stay available, up to a limit of 1 GB.
  • Sample library index. If you choose a folder of sounds, the App saves an index of that folder (file names, locations within the folder, and the sound type the App assigns to each file) so it does not need to scan the folder again each time.
  • Settings. Your preferences in the App.

4. Device Permissions

The App asks for the following permissions only when you use a feature that needs them. You can grant or withdraw each permission at any time in Settings › TuneGod on your iPhone; the rest of the App continues to work without them.

Microphone

Used only to record audio (for example, vocal takes) into your projects while you are recording. Recordings are stored on your device. The App does not record in the background and does not transmit audio.

Bluetooth

Used only to connect Bluetooth MIDI keyboards, pad controllers and game controllers. The App does not use Bluetooth to determine your location or to identify you or other people.

Files and folders

When you choose a sample library folder with the iOS file picker, iOS grants the App ongoing access to that folder, and only that folder, so your library is available each time you open the App. The App only reads audio files in that folder, on your device; it does not modify, upload or share them. You can end this access at any time by choosing a different folder in the App or by deleting the App.

When you open a single project file, the App reads that file once and does not keep ongoing access to it.

5. How Information Is Used

Information stored on your device is used only to provide the App's features, such as playing, editing, recording and saving your music. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose, and we have no access to it.

6. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade or otherwise share personal information, because we do not collect any.

If you choose to export or share a project or sound, the App uses the standard iOS share sheet. You decide where it goes (for example, Files, AirDrop, Messages or Mail), and the app or service you choose handles it under its own privacy policy.

7. Third-Party Services

The App does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software.

  • Apple. The App is distributed through Apple's App Store, which handles downloads and purchases under Apple's Privacy Policy. If you have turned on Share With App Developers in your iPhone's Analytics & Improvements settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and diagnostic data. You can turn this off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.
  • Device backups. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, the App's data may be included in that backup. Backups are managed by Apple and by your device settings, not by us.
  • Audio plug-ins. The App can load audio effects and instruments (Audio Unit extensions) that you have installed from other developers. Those extensions are governed by their developers' own privacy policies.

8. Tracking, Advertising and Analytics

The App does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, does not use the advertising identifier, does not show advertisements and does not use cookies or similar technologies.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not retain any. Information stored on your device stays there until you delete it in the App or delete the App, which removes the App's data from your device (copies in your own device backups are removed according to your backup settings).

10. Data Security

Information created by the App is kept in the App's storage on your device and is protected by the security features of iOS, including the data protection provided by your device passcode. No information is transmitted by the App, so none is exposed in transit.

11. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Because the App collects no personal information, it can be used by people of any age without any information being sent to us.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), including the right to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your personal information, and the right to opt out of its sale or sharing.

Because we do not collect, store or sell personal information, we hold no personal information about you to access, correct, delete or transfer. You keep full control of the information stored on your device and can delete it at any time. If you have a question about your rights, please contact us.

13. International Users

The App works the same way in every country. Because no information is transmitted to us, no personal information is transferred across borders by the App.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if a new feature changes how the App handles information. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date, before the version of the App they apply to is released. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
