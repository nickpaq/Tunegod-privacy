# TuneGod Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 1, 2026

TuneGod is a music-making app for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version

**TuneGod does not collect, store, sell or share any of your personal data.** The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no servers of its own. Everything you make in TuneGod stays on your device unless you choose to share it.

## What stays on your device

- **Your music and projects.** Sounds, patterns, recordings, favourites and projects (`.tg` files) are saved in the app's storage on your iPhone, or in folders you pick yourself. TuneGod never uploads them.
- **Settings.** Your preferences are saved on your device in the app's settings storage.
- **Your sample library folder.** When you choose a folder of sounds, iOS gives TuneGod lasting access to that folder, and only that folder. TuneGod keeps that access so your library is ready each time you open the app, and it reads the folder's audio files only on your device. It also saves an index of the sounds (file names and the sound type TuneGod gave each one) on your device, so it doesn't have to scan the folder again. You can end this access at any time by choosing a different folder in the app or by deleting TuneGod. The folder and its files are never changed, uploaded or shared.
- **Project files you open.** When you open a single project file with the iOS file picker, TuneGod reads that file once, on your device, and does not keep access to it.

If your iPhone backs up app data to iCloud or to a computer, that backup is handled by Apple under your device settings and Apple's privacy policy, not by TuneGod.

## Permissions TuneGod asks for

- **Microphone:** only so you can record vocal takes and other audio into your projects. Recordings stay on your device. TuneGod does not listen in the background and does not send audio anywhere.
- **Bluetooth:** only to connect Bluetooth MIDI keyboards, pad controllers and game controllers. TuneGod does not use Bluetooth to find your location or to identify you.

You can turn off either permission at any time in **Settings › TuneGod** on your iPhone. The rest of the app keeps working without them.

## Sharing

TuneGod sends nothing over the internet. If you export or share a project or a sound, you choose where it goes through the standard iOS share sheet (for example Files, AirDrop or Mail), and the app or service you pick handles it under its own privacy policy.

## Audio plug-ins

TuneGod can load audio effects and instruments (Audio Unit extensions) installed on your device. Those come from other developers and work under their own privacy policies.

## Children

TuneGod collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

## Changes to this policy

If TuneGod's handling of data ever changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change before the new version of the app comes out.
